# Investigation Workflow

1. **Read AGENTS.md:** Always align with safety guardrails first.
2. **Review Relevant Memory:** Check decisions, glossary, and current phase context.
3. **Identify Evidence Sources:** Determine which databases, files, or reports hold the truth.
4. **Perform Read-Only Inspection:** Execute SQL queries and parse stats without writing.
5. **Form Hypotheses:** Pinpoint potential causes of discrepancies or bugs.
6. **Validate Hypotheses:** Cross-check multiple data vectors and sample manually.
7. **Produce Findings:** Generate a detailed audit log or report.
8. **Recommend Actions:** Suggest changes or next phases without applying them yet.
