# Book Intelligence Audit

This audit evaluates the information structure of the key books in our library.

### 1. Malt Whisky Yearbook 2019 (Ingvar Ronde)
- **Provides:** Detailed distillery history, region, country, ownership, founded year, website links.
- **Coverage Potential:** High for distillery attributes and regional boundary mappings.
- **Automation Difficulty:** Low (Highly structured, tabular listings).

### 2. The World Atlas of Whisky (Dave Broom)
- **Provides:** Tasting notes, regional maps, brand lists, expression lists, ABV, and Age.
- **Coverage Potential:** High for Speyside, Islay, and Highland core ranges.
- **Automation Difficulty:** Medium (Requires text segment parsing).

### 3. Jim Murray's Whisky Bible 2020
- **Provides:** Ratings, tasting notes, release years, ABV, and Age.
- **Coverage Potential:** Extremely high volume of niche and special releases.
- **Automation Difficulty:** High (Heavily stylized language and shorthand notations).

### 4. The Flavour of Whisky (David Wishart)
- **Provides:** 7-axis flavor profiles, scientific distillery classifications.
- **Coverage Potential:** Core Scotch single malts (86 distilleries).
- **Automation Difficulty:** Low (Tabular structure).
