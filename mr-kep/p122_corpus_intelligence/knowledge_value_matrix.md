- **The Complete Whiskey Course**: Contemporary educational production+tasting course — accessible production primer.
- **The Famous Grouse whisky companion   her**: Brand-history companion (Famous Grouse) — single-brand depth.
- **The Whisky Tasting Guide**: Tasting-method primer (Graham Moore).
- **The flavour of whisky**: Wishart statistical/chemical treatment of flavor — only quantitative flavor-science source in corpus.
- **The ultimate book of whiskey ; over 300 **: 300+ single malts survey — broad but overlapping with Bible/Atlas.
